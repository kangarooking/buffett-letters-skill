  type: principle

  summary: |

    这是巴菲特从合伙基金时期到伯克希尔时期始终不变的核心承诺。

    股东凭什么信任他们？巴菲特从合伙基金时期起就将几乎全部净资产投入其中。

- id: p04
  title: 以相对业绩而非绝对数字衡量表现
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1963年信（Ground Rules）
  source_quote: |
    "Whether we do a good job or a poor job is not to be measured by whether we are plus or minus for the year.
    It is instead to be measured against the general experience in securities."
  summary: |
    投资业绩的评判标准不是绝对涨跌，而是相对于市场基准（如道琼斯指数/S&P 500）的表现。
    三年是评估的最低时限，五年更优。如果三年以上的滚动表现持续落后于基准，就应考虑更换投资管理人。
  tags: [principle, performance-measurement, ground-rules]

- id: p05
  title: 不做市场预测
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1963年信（Ground Rules）
  source_quote: |
    "I am not in the business of predicting general stock market or business fluctuations. If you think I can do this,
    or think it is essential to an investment program, you should not be in the partnership."
  summary: |
    短期的市场和宏观经济预测是没用的——"预测可能告诉你很多关于预测者的信息，但告诉你关于未来的什么都没有"。
    专注于企业分析，不要试图猜测市场方向。
  tags: [principle, forecasting, ground-rules]

- id: p06
  title: 保留收益的检验标准
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1983年信（Owner-Related Business Principles）
  source_quote: |
    "We test the wisdom of retaining earnings by assessing whether retention, over time, delivers shareholders
    at least $1 of market value for each $1 retained."
  summary: |
    公司是否应该保留收益而非分红的检验标准：每保留$1收益，必须最终为股东创造至少$1的市场价值。
    以五年为滚动周期评估。如果做不到，收益就应该分配给股东让他们自己配置。
  tags: [principle, capital-allocation, dividends]

- id: p07
  title: 不用杠杆
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 2017年信
  source_quote: |
    "This table offers the strongest argument I can muster against ever using borrowed money to own stocks.
    There is simply no telling how far stocks can fall in a short period."
  summary: |
    伯克希尔历史上有4次37%-59%的股价大跌。杠杆在顺利时放大收益，但一次灾难足以归零。
    即使借款很小、仓位不面临爆仓，市场暴跌时的恐惧新闻和不理性情绪也会扰乱心智，导致错误决策。
    永远不要用借来的钱持有股票。
  tags: [principle, risk, leverage]

- id: p08
  title: 恐惧与贪婪逆向操作
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1986年信
  source_quote: |
    "We simply attempt to be fearful when others are greedy and to be greedy only when others are fearful."
  summary: |
    当市场极度乐观时保持恐惧，当市场极度悲观时变得贪婪。这是一条行为原则——利用市场情绪而非被它驱使。
    巴菲特在2008年金融危机中再次重申："Be fearful when others are greedy, and be greedy when others are fearful."
  tags: [principle, market-psychology, contrarian]

- id: p09
  title: 资本配置第一法则——价格决定对错
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 2011年信
  source_quote: |
    "The first law of capital allocation – whether the money is slated for acquisitions or share repurchases –
    is that what is smart at one price is dumb at another."
  summary: |
    在某一价格下聪明的做法，在另一价格下可能是愚蠢的。价格是判断一切资本配置行为的终极标尺。
    无论是收购企业还是回购股票，都必须考虑具体价格。CEO在做回购决策前应先声明这一法则。
  tags: [principle, capital-allocation, price]

- id: p10
  title: 不发行低估的股票
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1983年信（Owner-Related Business Principles）
  source_quote: |
    "We will issue common stock only when we receive as much in business value as we give. This rule applies to
    all forms of issuance - not only mergers or public stock offerings, but stock for-debt swaps, stock options,
    and convertible securities as well."
  summary: |
    只在获得等值商业价值时才发行股票。发行股票等于出售公司的一部分，绝不能以低于企业整体价值的价格出售。
    这条规则适用于所有形式的股票发行——合并、公开发行、债转股、期权、可转债无一例外。
  tags: [principle, capital-allocation, stock-issuance]

- id: p11
  title: 以企业所有者视角投资
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1983年信（Owner-Related Business Principles）
  source_quote: |
    "Although our form is corporate, our attitude is partnership. Charlie Munger and I think of our shareholders as
    owner-partners, and of ourselves as managing partners."
  summary: |
    虽然公司形式是法人实体，但态度应该是合伙人关系。管理者是替股东管理资金的受托人，
    公司只是股东持有资产的管道。做决策时始终问：如果角色互换，我希望知道什么？
  tags: [principle, ownership-mentality, partnership]

- id: p12
  title: 只与喜欢和钦佩的人合作
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1989年信
  source_quote: |
    "We would rather achieve a return of X while associating with people whom we strongly like and admire
    than realize 110% of X by exchanging these relationships for uninteresting or unpleasant ones."
  summary: |
    宁可获得100%的回报与喜欢和钦佩的人合作，也不要为了110%的回报与令人不快的人共事。
    这不仅最大化了好结果的概率，也确保了过程本身的愉悦。工作与不喜欢的人合作就像"为了钱而结婚——
    无论怎样都是个坏主意"。
  tags: [principle, people, partnerships]

- id: p13
  title: 会计结果不影响经营决策
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1983年信（Owner-Related Business Principles）
  source_quote: |
    "Accounting consequences do not influence our operating or capital-allocation decisions. When acquisition costs

  summary: |
    会计数字不应驱动经营或资本配置决策。在收购成本相同时，宁买$2不可报告收益而非$1可报告收益。
    真正重要的是经济现实，而非会计呈现。
  tags: [principle, accounting, capital-allocation]

- id: p14
  title: 拒绝过度杠杆，宁可错过机会
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1983年信（Owner-Related Business Principles）
  source_quote: |
    "We will reject interesting opportunities rather than over-leverage our balance sheet.
    This conservatism has penalized our results but it is the only behavior that leaves us comfortable."
  summary: |
    伯克希尔很少使用大量债务；即使使用，也尽量采用长期固定利率。宁可错过有吸引力的机会，
    也不过度杠杆化资产负债表。这种保守虽然可能损失一些收益，但对受托管理保单持有人、储户、
    贷款人和大量净投入股东的资产管理者来说，这是唯一令人安心的行为。
  tags: [principle, risk, leverage, debt]

- id: p15
  title: CEO是首席风险官——不可委托
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 2008年信 & 2022年信
  source_quote: |
    "A CEO must not delegate risk control. It's simply too important."
    "Our CEO will always be the Chief Risk Officer – a task it is irresponsible to delegate."
  summary: |
    风险控制是CEO的核心职责，不能委托给风险委员会或首席风险官。如果CEO无法承担这个责任，
    就应该另谋高就。如果失职导致政府不得不介入救助，CEO和董事会应承担严重的财务后果。
    在伯克希尔，巴菲特亲自发起和监控每一份衍生品合约。
  tags: [principle, risk, CEO, governance]

- id: p16
  title: 坚守能力圈
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1999年信
  source_quote: |
    "If we have a strength, it is in recognizing when we are operating well within our circle of competence
    and when we are approaching the perimeter."
  summary: |
    投资成功的关键不是你知道多少，而是你如何切实际地定义自己不知道什么。
    只投资于你真正理解的企业——相对简单且业务特征稳定的企业。如果企业复杂或处于持续变化中，
    就不要自作聪明去预测未来现金流。投资者只需做对很少的事情，只要避免大错误。
  tags: [principle, circle-of-competence, discipline]

- id: p17
  title: 坚持安全边际
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1992年信
  source_quote: |
    "We insist on a margin of safety in our purchase price. If we calculate the value of a common stock to be
    only slightly higher than its price, we're not interested in buying. We believe this margin-of-safety principle,
    so strongly emphasized by Ben Graham, to be the cornerstone of investment success."
  summary: |
    只在价格显著低于计算出的价值时买入。如果价值仅略高于价格，就不感兴趣。
    安全边际是格雷厄姆强调的投资成功的基石——为核心投资原则，而非可选策略。
  tags: [principle, margin-of-safety, valuation]

- id: p18
  title: 真正的保守来自知识和理性，而非从众
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1961年信
  source_quote: |
    "You will not be right simply because a large number of people momentarily agree with you.
    You will not be right simply because important people agree with you...
    True conservatism is only possible through knowledge and reason."
  summary: |
    多数人同意不代表你对了，重要人物同意也不代表你对了。买入蓝筹股而不顾估值不是保守而是投机。
    真正的保守来自于正确的假设、正确的事实和正确的推理。检验保守性的客观标准是下行市场中的表现。
  tags: [principle, conservatism, independent-thinking]

- id: p19
  title: CEO误导公众最终会误导自己
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1983年信（Owner-Related Business Principles）
  source_quote: |
    "We also believe candor benefits us as managers: the CEO who misleads others in public
    may eventually mislead himself in private."
  summary: |
    对股东的坦诚报告不仅是义务，也是管理者自身的保护机制。CEO如果在公开场合误导他人，
    最终可能在私下里也误导自己。巴菲特承诺以"角色互换"的标准向股东披露信息——
    如果自己是股东，希望知道什么就告诉股东什么。
  tags: [principle, candor, reporting, governance]

- id: p20
  title: 不卖优秀企业——永久持有
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1983年信（Owner-Related Business Principles）
  source_quote: |
    "Regardless of price, we have no interest at all in selling any good businesses that Berkshire owns,
    and are very reluctant to sell sub-par businesses as long as we expect them to generate at least some cash."
  summary: |
    拥有优秀企业时不因价格卖出，这是伯克希尔区别于"gin rummy"式管理（每轮丢弃最差业务）的关键原则。
    宁可让整体业绩受到一点拖累，也不做频繁买卖的投机者。对于有优秀管理层的优秀企业，
    永久持有是默认策略。
  tags: [principle, holding-period, permanent-ownership]

- id: p21
  title: 不做蛤蟆变王子的幻想——好价格买好企业
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1981年信 & 1987年信
  source_quote: |
    "Many acquisition-hungry managers were apparently mesmerized by their childhood reading of the story about
    the frog-kissing princess... making silk purses out of silk is the best that we can do; with sow's ears, we fail."
  summary: |
    不要期待通过收购和管理层改造来把平庸企业变成优秀企业。亲吻蛤蟆只在童话里有效。
    用丝绸做丝线钱包是能做到的最好结果，但猪耳朵永远变不成丝线钱包。应该以合理价格买入优秀企业，
    而非以便宜价格买入平庸企业。
  tags: [principle, acquisitions, quality]

- id: p22
  title: 保险承保三条纪律
  type: checklist
  source_chapter: 2001年信
  source_quote: |
    "The winners are those that unfailingly stick to three key principles:
    1. They accept only those risks that they are able to properly evaluate (staying within their circle of competence).
    2. They limit the business they accept in a manner that guarantees they will suffer no aggregation of losses from
    a single event or from related events that will threaten their solvency.
    3. They avoid business involving moral risk: No matter what the rate, trying to write good contracts with bad
    people doesn't work."
  summary: |
    保险承保成功的三条核心纪律：（1）只接受能够正确评估的风险（在能力圈内），不考虑市场份额，
    不与愚蠢定价的竞争者比拼；（2）限制业务量，确保不会因单一或关联事件聚集亏损而威胁偿付能力；
    （3）避开道德风险——无论费率多高，与坏人签好合同行不通。
  tags: [checklist, insurance, underwriting, discipline]

- id: p23
  title: 对抗机构强制力——不因同行压力做蠢事
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 2004年信
  source_quote: |
    "Most American businesses harbor an 'institutional imperative' that rejects extended decreases in volume.
    What CEO wants to report to his shareholders that not only did business contract last year but that it will
    continue to drop?"
  summary: |
    大多数企业存在一种"机构强制力"：管理者不愿报告业务下滑，于是宁可做蠢事（如降价抢量）来维持增长假象。
    在保险业尤为危险——愚蠢定价的保单后果可能多年后才暴露。抵抗这种从众冲动需要极度自律。
    National Indemnity在行业低迷期宁可业务量大幅萎缩，也不降价跟风。
  tags: [principle, institutional-imperative, independent-thinking]

- id: p24
  title: 管理者的愿望清单不应以股东为代价
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 1983年信（Owner-Related Business Principles）
  source_quote: |
    "A managerial 'wish list' will not be filled at shareholder expense. We will not diversify by purchasing

  summary: |
    管理者不得为了个人野心而以牺牲股东利益为代价进行多元化收购。用股东的钱和用自己的钱一样谨慎——
    充分考量股东自己通过股票市场分散投资可以获得的性价比。不会以控股股东溢价去买对股东不划算的生意。
  tags: [principle, capital-allocation, shareholder-interest]

- id: p25

  type: principle

    现金虽然不会让人兴奋，但"钱在伯克希尔口袋里烧个洞也比舒舒服服待在别人口袋里好"。
    这种储备是应对极端不确定性的终极保障。
  tags: [principle, cash-reserve, risk-management]

- id: p26

  type: principle

  source_quote: |

  summary: |

    "唯一能理解的是我们不理解这家机构承担了多少风险"。衍生品已经从瓶子里放了出来，
    会不断增殖直到某个事件让它们的毒性暴露。伯克希尔应该成为金融力量的堡垒。
  tags: [principle, derivatives, risk, warning]

- id: p27
  title: EBITDA是危险指标——折旧是真实成本
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 2000年信

    "References to EBITDA make us shudder — does management think the tooth fairy pays for capital expenditures?"

  tags: [principle, accounting, EBITDA, valuation]
